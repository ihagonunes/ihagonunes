A productivity app exploring the relationship between **time, energy and attention**.

### 🏥 Asclépio

**Turning environmental data into actionable information.**

An IoT monitoring system for hospital environments, combining sensors, data processing and operational rules.

It monitors variables such as **CO₂, VOCs and occupancy**, transforming them into an environmental score and SLA-based alerts.
